    Offline PC Relay Deployment Guide

    Your target PC has no internet connection. It pulls and installs updates silently using this PowerShell command:

    powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\UpdateScripts\pull_update.ps1"

    The client script is available at client_scripts\pull_update.ps1. It checks latest_version.txt and installs .msi, .exe, or .zip packages silently in the background under Admin.
